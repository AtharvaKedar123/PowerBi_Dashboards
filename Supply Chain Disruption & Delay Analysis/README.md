## 📌 Overview

This dashboard analyzes **supply chain disruptions and delivery delays** to identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies.

It provides insights into **delay reasons, regional disruptions, and supplier performance**, helping optimize logistics operations.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- ⏱️ **Delay Tracking & Analysis**
- 🌍 **Region-Wise Disruption Insights**
- 📊 **Supplier Performance Evaluation**
- ⚠️ **Risk & Bottleneck Identification**
- 🚦 **On-Time vs Delayed Shipment Analysis**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

- Certain regions experience higher **delivery delays due to external factors**  
- Supplier performance varies significantly, impacting **delivery timelines**  
- Identifying bottlenecks helps reduce **logistics inefficiencies**

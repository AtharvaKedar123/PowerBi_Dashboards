## 📌 Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of **sales performance, revenue trends, and regional distribution**.

It helps businesses understand **sales growth, customer behavior, and product performance**, enabling data-driven decisions.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- 💰 **Revenue & Profit Analysis**
- 📦 **Sales Volume Tracking**
- 🌍 **Region-Wise Sales Performance**
- 🏆 **Top Products & Customer Insights**
- 📈 **Sales Trend Analysis Over Time**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

- A small group of products drives a large share of **total revenue**  
- Certain regions consistently outperform others in **sales growth**  
- Sales trends reveal opportunities for **strategic expansion**

## 📌 Overview

This dashboard analyzes **employee attrition and performance trends** to provide insights into workforce dynamics.

It helps organizations understand **why employees leave, how performance varies, and where improvements are needed**.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- 📉 **Employee Attrition Analysis**
- 📊 **Performance Tracking Across Departments**
- 🧑‍💼 **Department-Wise Workforce Insights**
- ⏳ **Tenure & Experience Analysis**
- 📈 **Employee Satisfaction & Trends**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

- Higher attrition is observed in specific **departments and tenure groups**  
- Employee performance varies significantly across **teams and roles**  
- Identifying trends helps improve **retention and workforce planning**
